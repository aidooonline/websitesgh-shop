01How your shop makes money

Someone finds a blender, taps WhatsApp, you agree a price, a rider delivers it, they pay cash. That is the whole business.

That third step is the thing that makes your shop unusual. The sale happens in a chat, not on the website. So Google and Meta can see somebody clicked, but they cannot see whether that person ever bought. They are flying blind, and so are you.

02The one number everything hangs on

When you sell a blender for GHS 400, that 400 is not yours. Here is where it goes.

money in money out yours

Made-up numbers, to show the shape.

That last figure has a name: profit per order. It is the most important number in your business, and every decision this system makes is really the same question.

Is an order worth more than it costs to get?

If a customer leaves you GHS 100 and an ad brings one in for GHS 60, spend more. If the ad costs GHS 140, you are paying to lose money, and selling harder makes it worse. Same ad, same price, opposite answer, and the only thing that decides it is that one number.

03Why this was worth building at all

You are running a shop where the sale disappears into WhatsApp. Without something watching, you can never answer three questions that decide whether the business grows:

1
Which ads actually brought a paying customer?Not clicks. Not messages. Money.
2
Which products actually make you money?The best seller can be the one you earn least on.
3
Do people come back?The second sale costs nothing in advertising. It is the cheapest money in retail.

Guessing at these is how a shop spends a year busy and flat.

06Your part

One thing, and it is a screen now, not a command.

In WordPress, go to WooCommerce › Product Costs. For the products that have sold, type what you pay the supplier and what the rider costs. Press Save.

Leave anything you do not know blank. Blank means "unknown" and the system skips it. A zero would tell it the product is pure profit, and it would then give you confident advice built on a lie.

The margin appears beside each box as you type. If a number is wrong, it goes red and says so before you have finished typing it.

You do not need all fifty. The handful that actually sell are what move the numbers.
