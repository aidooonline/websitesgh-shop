# Research: personal care tools in Ghana

Date: 24 July 2026.

## The angle
Heat again. A 2000W hair dryer at 10 min/day costs about GHS 20.37/month; a
45W straightener costs about GHS 0.46. The 40x spread between tools that both
"do hair" is a story nobody tells, and it sells the straightener.

| Tool | Draw | Typical use | GHS/month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hair dryer 2000W | 2000W | 10 min | 20.37 |
| Facial steamer | 300W | 10 min | 3.06 |
| Straightener | 45W | 10 min | 0.46 |
| Clipper (recharge) | 10W | 15 min | 0.15 |

## Buyer questions
1. Why does my dryer trip the breaker? (2000W + other loads; amps explainer.)
2. Clipper for home use vs barber use? (Duty cycle and blade metal.)
3. Ceramic vs metal straightener plates? (Ceramic heats evenly, kinder to hair.)
