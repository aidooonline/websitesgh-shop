# Research: audio in Ghana

Date: 24 July 2026.

## The angle
Battery life claims on budget earbuds are quoted at 50 per cent volume; real
use at high volume roughly halves them. Sell with the honest number: "about 3
hours loud, 5 quiet" beats "6H PLAYTIME" for trust and returns fewer units.

## Buyer questions
1. Why do my earbuds die faster than advertised? (Volume, and case top-ups.)
2. One earbud stopped pairing? (Reset ritual: forget, re-case, hold both.)
3. Speaker for outdooring vs indoor use? (Battery and volume headroom.)
