# Research: school bags and sets in Ghana

Date: 24 July 2026.

## The deadline that sells this category
GES confirmed 2026/27 term one: basic schools resume **Tuesday 8 September
2026**, SHS/SHTS **18 September 2026**. Every parent buys before those dates.
Window: mid August to 8 September. This is a countdown category and the copy
should say the date, not "back to school soon".

## Market pricing observed (Jiji Accra, July 2026)
- 5-in-1 set (bag, bottle, lunch bag, ice pack): GHS 230
- 3-in-1 set (bag, lunch bag, pencil case): GHS 220 to 230
- Single kids bag lower primary: GHS 110 to 130
- Basic school bag all sizes: GHS 120
Our placeholders sit inside this observed band.

## Sizing rule (the useful content nobody writes)
- Lower primary (age 4 to 8): small single bag, light frame, chest strap
- Upper primary and JHS: full size set, padded straps
- SHS boarder: trunk-adjacent needs, bag is secondary; laptop bag if day student
Bag weight rule of thumb: a packed bag should stay under roughly 10 to 15 per
cent of the child's body weight. Give the rule, parents remember the shop.

## Buyer questions
1. Which size for which class? 2. Waterproof or not for September rains?
3. What is in the 5-in-1 exactly? 4. Will it arrive before school opens?
(Answer with the date and the same-day Accra promise.)
