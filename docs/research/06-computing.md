# Research: computing accessories in Ghana

Date: 24 July 2026.

## The angle
Flash drive capacity fraud is this category's version of the wattage lie: fake
high capacity drives report 128GB to the OS but corrupt beyond their real 8 or
16GB. The honest test: fill it completely once and read every file back (h2testw
does this automatically). We publish the test and promise every drive we sell
passes it. That claim converts because everyone in Accra has been burned once.

## Buyer questions
1. Why did my 128GB drive corrupt my files? (Fake capacity, explain the test.)
2. Wireless mouse lag? (2.4G dongle vs Bluetooth, interference.)
3. Does a cooling pad actually help? (Modest, real for laptops on laps/beds.)
