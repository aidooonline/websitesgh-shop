# Video strategy: shoot on your camera, boost through Google Ads

## Your instinct is right, and here is the mechanism

Boosting a YouTube video THROUGH Google Ads (a Video campaign / Demand Gen)
does reach further and cheaper than boosting inside YouTube's own "promote"
button, because you get Google's full targeting, the retargeting audiences,
and better placement control. In 2026 the campaign types that place video are
Video campaigns and Demand Gen (Discovery and Video Action are retired).

BUT the same rule as TikTok applies: your model has no in-app checkout, so a
video ad's job is to drive the viewer to WhatsApp or to a product page, not to
close in-app. So video is awareness + retargeting fuel, and the SALE still
happens on WhatsApp. Weight video as a portfolio investment that fills your
pools and makes your Search brand terms grow, not as a last-click profit
centre.

## How video fits the funnel

1. Organic: post the clips free on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels, Status.
2. Boost the winners: take the clip that already performed organically and run
   it as a Google Video campaign or Demand Gen, targeting Ghana + your
   retargeting audiences + in-market appliance shoppers.
3. Retarget: everyone who watched 50%+ becomes an audience your Search and
   Demand Gen ads follow up cheaply.
4. Close: all roads end at WhatsApp.

Never boost a clip that flopped organically. Organic is the audition.

## The content angle that converts for YOU

Your brand is "we check the numbers." That is a scroll-stopping hook because it
is a mini-myth-buster, and myth-busting is one of the highest-retention video
formats. Every video takes ONE claim and disproves or proves it in 20-40
seconds, ending with the honest number and "pay on delivery, we deliver in
Accra."

## Shot list: 10 launch videos (shoot on your phone at the dealer/China Mall)

Each is 20-40 seconds, vertical 9:16, shot on your camera. Format for each:
HOOK (3s) -> PROOF (15-25s) -> OFFER (5s).

1. THE BLENDER WATTAGE ONE (your flagship)
   Hook: "This blender box says 8000 watts. That is impossible in Ghana."
   Proof: hold the box, point at the number, show a socket: "A Ghana socket
   gives 2,990 watts max. 8000 would need 35 amps, the fuse blows first."
   Offer: "We sell the real ones. Pay on delivery in Accra."

2. POWER BANK REAL CAPACITY
   Hook: "Your 30,000mAh power bank is lying to you. Here is the truth."
   Proof: "It delivers about 15,500 to your phone, not 30,000. Physics: cells
   are 3.7 volts, your phone takes 5. The rest is lost as heat."
   Offer: "We tell you the real charge count. Order on WhatsApp."

3. BLENDER LIVE TEST: blend pepper/tomatoes on camera, real speed, real jar.
   Hook: "Can a GHS 280 blender actually grind Ghana pepper? Let us see."

4. KETTLE SPEED TEST: time a full boil on camera.
   Hook: "How fast does a real 2L kettle boil? I timed it."

5. WHAT PAY ON DELIVERY LOOKS LIKE: film a mock handover.
   Hook: "You do not pay us first. This is how we sell."
   Proof: rider hands box, customer opens, inspects, THEN pays.

6. UNBOXING A GENUINE UNIT: slow, close, show the seal, the plug, the box.
   Hook: "Genuine, new, sealed. Let me show you the difference."

7. SCHOOL BAG SET (seasonal, run before Sept 8):
   Hook: "School opens 8 September. This is the set that lasts the year."

8. IRON: DRY VS STEAM in 30 seconds, who each suits.
   Hook: "Dry iron or steam iron? Stop guessing."

9. EARBUDS HONEST BATTERY: "They say 6 hours. Real is about 3 loud. Here is why."

10. RECHARGEABLE FAN/LAMP FOR LIGHT-OFF:
    Hook: "Light off? This runs X hours on one charge. I tested it."

## Production rules for high conversion

- First 3 seconds MUST contain the claim or the number. No intros.
- Show the real product in your real hand. Authenticity beats polish here.
- One product, one claim, one price per video.
- Always end with the same 5 words of offer so it becomes your signature.
- Caption everything (most watch on mute). Big text, key number highlighted.
- Vertical 9:16 for Shorts/TikTok/Status; you can also export 16:9 for
  in-stream YouTube ads later.
- Put the price on screen. In Ghana the price IS the hook for a lot of buyers.

## Boosting settings (Google Video campaign)

- Campaign type: Video, or Demand Gen if you want Gmail/Discover too
- Objective: awareness/consideration at first (views + landing), then swap to
  the WhatsApp-tap conversion once the pixel has data
- Targeting: Ghana; layer your retargeting audiences + in-market "home
  appliances" + custom-intent audiences built from your Search keywords
- Format: skippable in-stream + in-feed; bumpers (6s cuts) for reach
- Budget: start tiny, $3-5/day per winning video, scale only what holds
  a low cost per view AND drives WhatsApp taps
