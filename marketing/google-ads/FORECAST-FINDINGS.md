# Keyword forecast findings, 25 July 2026

Source: Google Keyword Planner forecast, Ghana, Google Search, August 2026,
Maximize Conversions, $20/day budget, run against the 91-keyword v1 set.

## What it returned

| Metric | Forecast |
|---|---|
| Impressions per month | 630 |
| Clicks per month | 38 |
| Cost per month | $9.55 |
| Average CPC | $0.25 |
| Conversions per month | 3 |
| CPA | $2.84 |
| Mobile share of clicks | 97% |

## The headline

The budget was set to $20/day, which is $600 a month. The forecast can only
spend **$9.55** of it. **98.4% of the budget cannot be spent**, because there is
not enough search volume on these keywords to spend it.

This is the single most important number in the whole plan, and it changes the
channel strategy.

## What is good

The unit economics work. CPA $2.84 against roughly $8.75 profit per order keeps
68% of the margin. CPC came in at exactly the $0.25 estimated in the launch kit,
and CTR at 6% matched the 4 to 6% benchmark. Nothing about the account structure
is wrong.

## What is broken

Scale. A "conversion" in this forecast is a WhatsApp tap, not a sale. At a 30 to
50% tap-to-sale rate, 3 taps a month is **one to one and a half sales a month**.
That is not a business, it is a trickle.

It also breaks the milestone ladder: Smart Bidding needs about 30 conversions a
month to stabilise, and at 3 a month that gate is ten months away, so Target CPA
is unreachable on Search alone with this keyword set.

## Why the volume is missing

Three causes, in order of size:

1. **Too much exact match.** Exact only fires on near-identical queries. The v1
   set was heavily exact, which is excellent for precision and terrible for
   reach in a small market.
2. **Every keyword required a long modifier.** Real people type "blender ghana",
   not "blender price in ghana". The long forms are the lowest-volume variants
   of each idea.
3. **No brand coverage and no umbrella terms.** Ghanaians search "binatone
   blender" and "home appliances accra". v1 had zero of either.

Ghana's total search volume for small appliances is genuinely modest, so some of
this is the market. But the three causes above are self-inflicted and fixable.

## The correction: v2 keyword set

- 536 keywords across 18 ad groups, up from 91 across 14.
- Phrase is now the default; exact is kept only on the two strongest buy phrases
  per category.
- Short geo forms added throughout: "blender ghana", "blender accra".
- Brand ad groups added: binatone, silver crest, nasco, bruhm, sayona, saachi,
  nunix, sonik.
- Umbrella shopping terms added: "home appliances ghana", "buy appliances online
  ghana", "appliance shop accra".
- Two extra categories included: air fryers and deep fryers.

The 107 negatives stay exactly as they are. They are the guardrail that makes
broadening safe: this is a deliberate widening of reach, not a return to the
unqualified 621-keyword draft, because every keyword still carries a commercial
or geographic signal.

## What this means for the channel plan

Google Search is now correctly understood as a **small, cheap, profitable
trickle**, not the growth engine. It should run, because $2.84 CPA against $8.75
profit is good money, but it will not carry the business at this volume.

The growth has to come from the channels that create demand rather than harvest
it: organic video, TikTok, and the content clusters. That reverses the earlier
recommendation that Google should take the budget weight and TikTok stay lean.
The forecast is the reason.

## Budget correction

Do not set $20/day. The forecast says the whole month costs $9.55. Set **$1 to
$2 a day** and let it run; the campaign cannot spend more than the auction
offers. Revisit once the v2 set is forecast and, if volume rises, raise it to
match what is actually spendable.

## Next step

impressions and clicks against these figures. That comparison tells us whether
the volume ceiling is the market or the keyword set.
