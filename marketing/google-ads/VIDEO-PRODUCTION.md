# Video production: the exact style you described, and how to make it

## Your spec, confirmed by 2026 research

You described: product photos and your own short clips, mixed with light AI
visuals, motion graphics and on-screen text, music behind it, NO AI voice, NO
voiceover. That format has a name: a FACELESS product ad built on KINETIC
TYPOGRAPHY plus product visuals. The research is clear it works:

- Faceless ads sell "by the product, proof, interaction, screen flow, motion,
  not by a presenter." The product must appear early and the CTA must feel like
  the next natural step.
- Kinetic typography (animated text synced to music beats) is the workhorse of
  high-performing faceless content. Facebook and Instagram report 20-30% higher
  engagement on video vs static, and kinetic type delivers that without the
  cost of filming a presenter.
- Stock-footage slideshows are the LOWEST performing faceless format in 2026.
  So the difference between a video that works and one that flops is MOTION and
  RHYTHM, not fancy footage. Your real product clips + animated text + beat-
  synced cuts beat any stock montage.

## The winning structure (每 video, 15-30s, 9:16)

HOOK (0-3s): the claim or the price, as big animated text over the product.
   e.g. "8000W?" slams on screen, then "IMPOSSIBLE." over the blender.
PROOF (3-20s): the product from 2-3 angles (your clips/photos), each with one
   animated line of text. The number is the star. Beat-synced cuts.
OFFER (20-30s): price on screen, "Pay on delivery. Accra." and the WhatsApp
   handle. Same closing card every time = your signature.

## The 3-layer visual recipe (this is the "nice UI/UX on video" you want)

1. BASE: your real product footage or clean product photo on a solid brand
   background (green #0E8C5A or off-white). Slow push-in or slow pan gives life
   to a still photo (the Ken Burns move).
2. MOTION TEXT: big geometric sans-serif (Montserrat, Poppins, or your Figtree),
   one phrase at a time, animated in on the beat. Highlight the KEY NUMBER in
   gold. This is the layer that carries the message with no voice.
3. ACCENTS: subtle UI-style elements - a price tag that pops in, a check mark,
   an underline swipe, a circle drawn around the number. These micro-animations
   are what make it feel designed, not a slideshow.

Music: one upbeat, clean track, licensed. Cut your text hits to the beat. Align
key text to audio markers; even 2-frame adjustments improve the feel.

## Tools, cheapest to most capable (all do this style, no voice needed)

- CapCut (free, mobile or desktop): templates for kinetic text, beat detection,
  huge licensed music library. This alone makes your whole style. START HERE.
- Canva (free/pro): drag-drop motion text, brand kit for your colours/fonts,
  product background remover. Great for the UI-accent look.
- For light AI visuals you mix in (NO AI voice): use image-to-video on a
  product photo to get a slow cinematic move, or generate a clean background.
  Keep AI to backgrounds and motion, never faces or voice, so it stays honest.

## Where AI fits (and where it must NOT)

FIT: background generation, turning a product photo into a slow-moving shot,
upscaling, removing a messy background behind a product, generating a branded
pattern. These save time and look clean.
MUST NOT: AI voice (you said no, and for a trust brand it is the right call -
your whole angle is honesty), fake product footage that misrepresents the
item, AI faces pretending to be customers. If it fakes the product or a person,
it breaks the "we check the numbers" trust. Keep AI on the decoration, never on
the claim.

## Batch workflow (make 10 videos in one session)

1. Shoot once: go to the dealer/China Mall, film 20-30 seconds of each product
   from 2-3 angles, plus one "hero" still. Phone is fine. Good light only.
2. Template once: build ONE CapCut project with your hook-proof-offer structure,
   your closing WhatsApp card, your font and colours, your music.
3. Duplicate and swap: for each product, duplicate the template, drop in that
   product's clips, change the text lines and the number. 10 videos in an
   afternoon once the template exists.
4. Export 9:16 for Shorts/TikTok/Status. Export one 16:9 cut of your best 2-3
   for YouTube in-stream ads later.

## Post, watch, then boost (ties back to the ad plan)

Post all 10 organically first (free). Watch which get views and WhatsApp taps
in your Attribution dashboard. Only the proven winners get boosted through a
Google Video/Demand Gen campaign or a TikTok Spark ad. Organic is the audition;
paid backs the winners. Never boost a flop.

## The number rule that makes YOUR videos convert

Every video must put ONE number on screen and make it the hero: the real
wattage, the real mAh, the price, the boil time, the runtime. Your brand is
"we check the numbers" - so the number IS the creative. A video that just shows
a pretty blender is a slideshow. A video that shows "600W real, GHS 6.11/month,
GHS 280" is your brand, and it converts because it teaches while it sells.
