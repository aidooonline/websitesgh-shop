01   The Core Decision

Search campaigns, not Performance Max or Shopping.

Standard ecommerce runs Shopping + PMax. You should not, and the reason is structural. Shopping and PMax need on-site checkout with a product feed and a purchase conversion Google can read. Your orders happen on WhatsApp. Google cannot see a WhatsApp sale, so those campaign types would optimise on nothing and burn budget.

Your real conversion is a WhatsApp tap, which is a lead. For lead generation, every 2024-2026 dataset shows Search beats PMax on quality, because buyers use specific terms that need specific ad copy. So we run a tight Search campaign with the WhatsApp tap as the conversion, and upgrade to the offline SALE import once there is volume.

Revisit PMax only after checkout is a tracked path, or after Search is profitable and you want cross-channel scale.

02   Account Structure

Campaign: WGH Shop - Search - Appliances

  • Location: Greater Accra first. Expand to Tema, Kumasi, Takoradi once proven; nationwide only where the delivery promise holds.
  • Network: Search only. Turn OFF Search Partners and Display at launch, they leak budget.
  • Budget: GHS 150-170/day, about GHS 5,000/month.
  • Structure: 14 tight ad groups, one product theme each, each mapped to its own landing page. Tight structure raises Quality Score, and a high Quality Score can cut your real CPC by up to half. This is the single biggest cost lever.

06   Bidding Phases

1
Weeks 1-2: Maximise Clicks, CPC cap GHS 2.50. Gather data cheaply.
2
Weeks 3-4: Switch to Maximise Conversions once ~15-20 WhatsApp taps are logged.
3
Month 2+: Target CPA once the offline SALE import has volume, so Google optimises for paying customers, not tappers. Start target ~GHS 60-70 per sale against ~GHS 105 profit.

08   Video & YouTube Plan

You are right that boosting a YouTube video through Google Ads reaches further and cheaper than YouTube's own promote button, because you get full targeting, retargeting audiences, and placement control.

But the same rule as every channel applies here: no in-app checkout, so a video's job is to drive the viewer to WhatsApp. Video is awareness and retargeting fuel; the sale still closes on WhatsApp. Boost only clips that already performed organically. Organic is the audition.

The 10 launch videos (shoot on your camera at the dealer)

  1. Blender wattage truth - "This box says 8000W. Impossible in Ghana." Show the socket math.
  2. Power bank real capacity - "Your 30,000mAh delivers 15,500. Here is why."
  3. Blender live test - grind real pepper on camera.
  4. Kettle speed test - time a full boil.
  5. What pay-on-delivery looks like - film the handover and inspection.
  6. Genuine unboxing - show the seal, plug, box.
  7. School bag set - seasonal, run before 8 September.
  8. Dry vs steam iron - who each suits, in 30 seconds.
  9. Earbuds honest battery - "6 hours claimed, 3 real. Here is why."
  10. Rechargeable fan/lamp - "Light off? Runs X hours. I tested it."

Production rules

  • First 3 seconds must contain the claim or number. No intros.
  • Real product in your real hand. Authenticity beats polish.
  • One product, one claim, one price per video.
  • Same 5-word offer to close every video, so it becomes your signature.
  • Caption everything, most watch on mute. Highlight the key number.
  • Vertical 9:16 for Shorts/TikTok/Status; export 16:9 for in-stream ads later.
  • Put the price on screen. In Ghana the price is the hook.

Boost settings: Video or Demand Gen campaign, Ghana, layered with your retargeting audiences and in-market home appliances. Start GHS 30-50/day per winning video, scale only what holds a low cost per view and drives taps.

09   How To Import

  1. Open Google Ads Editor, download your account.
  2. Account > Import > From file > keywords-editor-import.csv
  3. Repeat for negative-keywords.csv and responsive-search-ads.csv
  4. Review proposed changes, set campaign budget and location.
  5. Post. Nothing spends until you set the campaign live.
