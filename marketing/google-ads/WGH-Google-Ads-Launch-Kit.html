01   The Leak & The 80% Rule

The rentals leak, and why it will not happen here.

A past client campaign bled money on rental traffic: the keywords promised something the landing page did not deliver, so clicks were paid for and nothing was gained. Bare head terms do the same to an appliance shop, "blender" pulls recipes, repairs, reviews and brand-hunters.

The 80% rule: a keyword earns its place only if a person typing it would be happy landing on your exact page. Three tests, all must pass, INTENT (a buyer, not a researcher or renter), PRODUCT (you stock the exact thing), GEO (Ghana, delivery, price shown).

Applying it cut the list from 621 keywords to 91. Fewer keywords, far higher qualified-click rate. Volume is the enemy of a lean budget.

02   Search, Not PMax

Standard ecommerce runs Shopping + Performance Max. You should not: both need on-site checkout with a purchase feed Google can read, and your sale happens on WhatsApp, which Google cannot see. They would optimise on nothing and burn budget.

Your conversion is a WhatsApp tap, a lead. For lead-gen, every 2024-2026 dataset shows Search beats PMax on quality. So: a tight Search campaign, WhatsApp tap as conversion, upgraded to the offline SALE import once there is volume.

05   The Weekly Ritual (where 80% is won)

Keywords are a starting guess. The search terms report is the truth. Nobody launches at 80%, you prune to it. Every week:

  1. Campaign > Insights > Search terms. Read every real query that triggered your ads.
  2. Any query that is NOT a real buyer for a product you stock: add as a negative immediately.
  3. Any query that IS a great buyer and converted: add as an exact keyword.
  4. Any ad group above ~GHS 105 cost-per-order for two weeks: pause, move budget to winners.

Four weeks of this and the account self-cleans toward 80%+ qualified traffic. The Attribution dashboard on the site shows cost-per-order per product.

07   Video: Your Style, Done Right

You want product visuals and your own clips, light AI on the decoration only, motion graphics and on-screen text, music, no AI voice, no voiceover. The 2026 research confirms this exact format: a faceless ad built on kinetic typography plus product visuals. It works, and it is cheap.

What the data says: faceless ads sell by the product and motion, not a presenter. Kinetic typography (animated text synced to music) drives 20-30% higher engagement than static. And stock-footage slideshows are the LOWEST performing format, so the difference is motion and rhythm, which your real clips + animated text deliver.

The 3-layer recipe (your "nice UI on video")

1
Base: your real product clip or photo on a brand background. A slow push-in gives a still photo life.
2
Motion text: big geometric font, one phrase at a time, animated on the beat, key NUMBER highlighted in gold. This carries the message with no voice.
3
Accents: a price tag that pops, a check mark, a circle drawn round the number. Micro-animations make it feel designed, not a slideshow.

Structure, every video, 15-30s, 9:16

Hook (0-3s): the claim or price as big text over the product. Proof (3-20s): 2-3 angles, one line of text each, the number is the star, cuts on the beat. Offer (20-30s): price + "Pay on delivery. Accra." + WhatsApp, same closing card every time.

Tools (no voice needed)

CapCut (free) is your whole style in one app: kinetic text templates, beat detection, licensed music. Start here. Canva for the UI-accent look and your brand kit. AI only for backgrounds, slow-motion on a photo, or background removal, never voice, faces, or faked product.

The number rule

Every video puts ONE number on screen as the hero: real wattage, real mAh, price, boil time, runtime. Your brand is "we check the numbers", so the number IS the creative. A pretty blender is a slideshow. "600W real, GHS 6.11/month, GHS 280" is your brand, and it teaches while it sells.

Batch it

Shoot all products in one dealer visit (2-3 angles each, phone is fine, good light). Build ONE CapCut template. Duplicate and swap clips + text + number per product. 10 videos in an afternoon. Post all organically first, boost only the winners.

08   Import Steps

  1. Open Google Ads Editor, download your account.
  2. Account > Import > From file > keywords-editor-import.csv
  3. Repeat for negative-keywords.csv and responsive-search-ads.csv
  4. Set location to Greater Accra, budget GHS 150-170/day, network Search only (Partners + Display OFF).
  5. Review, Post. Nothing spends until you set it live.
