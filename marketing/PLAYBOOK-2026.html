01   Why This Plan Changed

The forecast that reset everything

Google Keyword Planner, Ghana, Google Search, August 2026, run against the 91-keyword set at a $20/day budget, returned 630 impressions, 38 clicks, $9.55 cost and 3 conversions for the entire month.

The budget offered $600 a month. Only $9.55 of it can be spent. 98.4% of the budget has nowhere to go, because the search demand does not exist at that volume.

What was right: CPC came in at exactly $0.25 as estimated, CTR at 6%, and CPA at $2.84 against roughly $8.75 profit per order. The structure is sound. Only the scale is missing.

The conclusion is structural, not tactical: Ghana is not a search-first market for small appliances. People do not Google "blender price in ghana". They go to Melcom, they go to China Mall, they ask in a WhatsApp group, or they see something while scrolling. There is very little demand to harvest.

So the business has to create demand rather than harvest it. That single sentence reorders every channel below.

04   Meta Click-to-WhatsApp: The Primary Engine

Why this is the right primary channel

Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) are Facebook or Instagram ads that open a WhatsApp conversation directly. No form, no landing page redirect, no cookie dependency. The checkout problem that ruled out Google Shopping and Performance Max simply does not exist here, because the chat is the checkout.

Industry reporting through 2026 describes CTWA as the dominant acquisition channel for cash-on-delivery ecommerce in emerging markets. That is a precise description of this business: COD, WhatsApp, Ghana.

Compare that to Google Search's one to one and a half sales a month. The difference is not lead quality, which favours Google. It is that Meta has inventory to sell you and Google does not.

Step by step: launching your first CTWA campaign

  1. Prepare the WhatsApp Business account. Use the same number the shop already uses (0542148020 / 233542148020). Set the business profile, hours, and a catalogue if you have product photos. A bare profile costs you conversions.
  2. Connect WhatsApp to your Meta Business account. In Meta Business Suite, go to Settings, then WhatsApp Accounts, and link the number. Without this link the CTWA option will not appear and tracking will be incomplete.
  3. Create the campaign. Objective: Engagement, then destination WhatsApp. For your first campaign, optimise for Conversations Started. Do not start on Sales; you do not yet have the event volume for it.
  4. Set the audience. Location: Greater Accra first, then Tema. Age 22 to 45. Do NOT stack many interests at this budget; a narrow audience raises CPM. Start broad within Accra and let the algorithm find people.
  5. Placements: Advantage+ placements (automatic). At this spend, manual placement selection restricts delivery and raises cost.
  6. Budget: start at $5 a day for the first two weeks. That is enough to exit learning on a conversation objective without risking much.
  7. Creative: use a clip that already performed organically. Vertical video, product in hand, the number on screen. First three seconds must carry the claim or the price.
  8. The message template. Set the pre-filled WhatsApp opener to something specific, for example: "Hi, I saw the 2L blender at GHS 280. Is it available?" A specific opener converts far better than "Hi".
  9. Reply fast. The 72-hour free window starts at the click. Replies within minutes convert dramatically better than replies within hours. This is the single biggest operational lever on the whole channel.
  10. After 50 conversations, move the optimisation event from Conversations Started toward a purchase or qualified-lead event, so Meta stops finding people who are cheap to engage and starts finding people who buy.

The two traps that ruin CTWA campaigns

1. Lost attribution. Industry analysis in 2026 reports that nine out of ten CTWA advertisers see "conversations started" in their dashboard but cannot tie any of it to sales, because they never capture the ctwa_clid Meta injects, nor fire the matching Conversions API event. Your ref-code loop already ties chats to clicks, which puts you ahead, but the Meta side needs wiring.

2. Staying on message-starts too long. Meta optimises for whatever you ask for. Ask for conversations and it will find people who love starting conversations and never buy. Move to a purchase event as soon as you have the volume.

Setup, corrected

  1. Import the v3 keyword file in Google Ads Editor: Account, Import, From file. Then the negatives file, then the ads file. Review, then Post.
  2. Budget $2 a day, not $20. The campaign physically cannot spend more than the auction offers, and a large budget on a thin auction just looks alarming in reports.
  3. Location: Greater Accra, expanding to Tema, Kumasi and Takoradi only where delivery genuinely reaches.
  4. Network: Search only. Turn OFF Search Partners and Display; they leak budget on low-intent placements.
  5. Bidding: Maximise Clicks with a $0.30 CPC cap for the first three weeks. Do not attempt Target CPA. Smart Bidding needs roughly 30 conversions a month to stabilise and this channel will not produce that.
  6. Device: the forecast shows 97% of clicks are mobile. Check that every landing page is fast and clean on a phone before spending a cedi.
  7. Weekly ritual: read the Search Terms report, add junk queries as negatives, promote converting queries to exact keywords. Four weeks of this prunes the account toward 80% qualified traffic.

06   TikTok: The Minimum-Budget Trap

Read this before you open TikTok Ads Manager

TikTok Ads Manager enforces a minimum campaign budget of about $500, a $50 per day campaign minimum and a $20 per day ad group minimum. That is roughly $1,500 a month. It is out of reach at this budget, and trying to run it properly would consume the entire marketing spend on one channel.

The way round it: TikTok Promote, the in-app boost button on your own posts, has no such minimum and reportedly runs at $0.07 to $0.20 per click. Targeting is cruder, but at this spend level that trade is correct.

How to use TikTok properly on this budget

  1. Post organically, daily. Free. This is the audition.
  2. Watch which clip earns views and WhatsApp taps on its own. Your attribution screen shows the taps; TikTok shows the views.
  3. Promote only proven winners, $3 to $5 per clip, from the app. Never boost a clip that flopped organically; you are just buying a bigger flop.
  4. Objective: "More website visits" pointed at the product page, or "More messages" if available in Ghana. Track with UTMs so the dashboard can attribute it.
  5. Revisit Ads Manager only when total monthly ad spend can absorb $50 a day on one channel. At that point Spark Ads become worthwhile: 2026 benchmark datasets put Spark Ads CTR at roughly 1.9x standard in-feed, with about 28% lower CPA.

07   Demand Gen And YouTube

Add this third, once Meta is producing reliably. Boosting a YouTube video through a Google Demand Gen campaign gives full targeting, retargeting audiences and placement control, which the in-app YouTube promote button does not.

  • Objective: awareness or consideration first, then swap to the WhatsApp-tap conversion once the pixel has data.
  • Targeting: Ghana, layered with your retargeting audiences and custom-intent audiences built from the v3 Search keywords.
  • Budget: $3 to $5 a day per winning video, scaled only while cost per view holds and WhatsApp taps follow.
  • Format: vertical for Shorts, plus one 16:9 cut of your best clip for in-stream.

08   Organic Video: The Free Engine

Zero cost, starts today, and every paid channel depends on it. Paid money should only ever back something organic has already proven.

The format, confirmed by 2026 research

Faceless product ads built on kinetic typography: real product footage, animated text synced to music, no voiceover and no AI voice. Stock-footage slideshows are the lowest-performing faceless format, so the difference between working and flopping is motion and rhythm, not fancy footage.

The three-layer recipe

  1. Base: your real product clip or photo on a brand background. A slow push-in gives a still photo life.
  2. Motion text: big geometric font, one phrase at a time, animated on the beat, the key number highlighted in gold.
  3. Accents: a price tag that pops, a check mark, a circle drawn round the number. Micro-animations make it feel designed rather than a slideshow.

The structure, every clip, 15 to 30 seconds, 9:16

Hook (0-3s): the claim or the price as big text over the product. Proof (3-20s): two or three angles, one line of text each, the number is the star. Offer (20-30s): price, "Pay on delivery. Accra.", and the WhatsApp handle. Same closing card every time so it becomes your signature.

Tools: CapCut is the entire workflow, free, with beat detection and licensed music. Canva for the accent layer. AI is allowed for backgrounds and slow-motion on a still photo, and never for voice, faces, or faked product footage. Your brand is honesty; one faked frame destroys it.

The rule that protects the budget

10   Measurement: The One Thing That Must Not Break

Every channel above ends in the same place, a WhatsApp chat, which is invisible to every ad platform by default. The attribution engine already built into the shop is what makes the whole plan measurable.

What is already live

  • Click IDs (gclid, gbraid, wbraid) and UTMs captured into 90-day first-party cookies.
  • Every WhatsApp tap logged with product, price, placement and campaign.
  • A WG-XXXX reference code stamped into each message, so the code the customer sends is the row in your admin.
  • Name, phone and area captured once before the first WhatsApp tap and remembered afterwards.
  • Add-to-cart logged as a funnel stage, so the full path is measurable: view, cart, tap, sale.

What to add per channel

  1. UTM every single ad. No exceptions. utm_source=meta&utm_medium=ctwa&utm_campaign=blender-aug&utm_content=clip3. Without this the dashboard cannot separate channels.
  2. Meta: capture the ctwa_clid from the webhook and fire the matching Conversions API event, or you will see conversations with no sales attached.
  3. Google: create an offline conversion action named exactly WhatsApp Sale, then export confirmed sales weekly from the attribution screen and upload them. This is what teaches Smart Bidding to chase buyers rather than tappers.
  4. Weekly: mark delivered orders as Sold, export, upload. Consistency matters more than volume in the early months.

What is new versus the first set

  • Phrase is now the default, exact kept only on the two strongest buy phrases per category. Exact match only fires on near-identical queries and was throttling reach in a small market.
  • Short geo forms added: people type "blender ghana", not "blender price in ghana".
  • Payment and delivery intent as its own ad group: "pay on delivery ghana", "cash on delivery appliances ghana". These match your model exactly and almost nobody bids on them.
  • Problem-solution long tail: "blender grind pepper", "rice cooker for jollof", "iron for kente", "rechargeable fan for light off". Low volume, very high intent, and uncontested.
  • Brand ad groups: Binatone, Silver Crest, Nasco, Bruhm, Sayona, Saachi, Nunix, Sonik, Kenwood, Century.
  • Occasion and gifting: wedding, outdooring, Christmas, back to school, new apartment, hostel.
  • The 107 negatives are unchanged. They are the guardrail that makes this widening safe.
